# Sections

This page lists the sections you can add to your templates, with what each one is for and the blocks it uses.

To add a section, open a template in the theme editor, click **Add section**, and pick one from the list. Sections are grouped by category, as in the tables below. Every section works in every style.

Most sections share these settings: a heading and heading alignment, a **Color scheme**, and **Full width**. Sections with several items often offer a grid or carousel layout and separate column counts for large screens and mobile.

## Banners

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Image banner | A large image with text on top or, on mobile, below. Supports a separate mobile image, a link for the whole banner, heights from small to full screen, content position and an image overlay. Presets: **Image banner** and **For professionals**. | Heading, Text, Button, Group, Custom Liquid, apps |
| Slideshow | Up to five slides, each with its own desktop and mobile image, link and text position. Dots and arrows, slide or fade transition, and optional autoplay with a pause button. | Slide, with Heading, Text, Button and Countdown timer inside |
| Navigation slideshow | A slideshow beside a quick navigation panel built from a menu. Links to collections and products show their image, and product links can show a price. | Slide |

## Media

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Media with text | An image or video beside text. Set the media position, width and fit. Uploaded videos can play automatically, muted. | Any theme block, such as Heading, Text and Button, plus apps |
| Video | A video with a play button, or a background video with text on top. Uses an uploaded video or a YouTube or Vimeo URL, with a cover image. Presets: **Video** and **Background video**. Background videos play automatically only when uploaded to Shopify. | Any theme block, plus apps |
| Media grid | Up to 16 image or video tiles that can span several columns and rows, each with a subheading, heading and button. | Media |
| Logo list | Brand or press logos in a grid or carousel. Enter a brand name for each logo for screen readers. | Logo |
| Before and after | Two images with a slider to compare them. See [Hair care and body care](hair-and-body-care.md#sections). | None |

## Text

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Rich text | Text content with flexible blocks, positioned at the start, center or end. | Any theme block, plus apps |
| Multi-column | Columns with an optional image or video, a heading, text and a link. Up to 12 columns. | Column |
| Icons with text | Short items such as delivery, returns and secure payment, with theme icons or your own images. Up to eight items. | Item |
| Collapsible content | Questions and answers grouped under category headings. It can add FAQ structured data for search engines. | Question, Category, Button |
| Testimonials | Up to five quotes with star ratings, author names and images, shown one at a time. Optional autoplay with a pause button. | Testimonial |
| Scrolling banner | A continuously scrolling row of text, icons, images and buttons. It can pause on hover and stops for visitors who prefer reduced motion. | Item |

## Products

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Featured collection | Products from a collection as a grid or carousel, with an optional promotion tile and "View all" link. Add tabs to show a different collection in each tab. Presets: **Featured collection** and **Gift guide**. | Tab |
| Product list | Hand-picked products as a grid or carousel, with an optional promotion tile and link. | None |
| Featured product | One product with its gallery and product blocks, on any page. | Product blocks, plus apps |
| Product features | Numbered hotspots on a product image, each explaining a detail. Up to eight features. | Feature |
| Shoppable image | Hotspots on an image that open small product cards, for shop the look. Up to eight hotspots. | Hotspot |
| Product comparison grid | Chosen products side by side, with the rows you add. Scrolls sideways on small screens. | Row: price, vendor, product type, variant options, description, rating, weight, text or metafield |
| Reviews | The product's rating and your reviews app block, with links to all reviews and to write a review. | Apps, Custom Liquid |
| Related products | Shopify's product recommendations. Product templates only. | None |
| Recently viewed | Products the shopper viewed recently. | None |
| Guided finder | Questions whose answers open a filtered collection. See [Set up the guided finder](hair-and-body-care.md#set-up-the-guided-finder). | Question |
| Routine steps | Numbered steps, each with an optional product. Up to six steps. | Step |
| Ingredient spotlight | An image beside key ingredients with short explanations. Up to eight ingredients. | Ingredient |

## Product

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Product details | A full-width area under the product for tabs, notes, payment information, text and apps. Product templates only. | Any theme block, such as Product tabs, Highlight text, Payment methods and the hair care and body care blocks, plus apps |

## Collections

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Collection list | Chosen collections as cards, in a grid or carousel. Up to 16 collections, each with an optional replacement image. Card style comes from Theme settings > Collection cards. | Collection |

## Promotions

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Countdown timer | A banner with a countdown to a date and time you set, with an optional background image. | Heading, Text, Button, Countdown timer, Discount code |
| Promo grid | Up to 16 promotion tiles that span columns and rows, each with up to three lines of text and an optional circle. | Media |
| Promo strip | A slim band for a short offer. | Heading, Text, Button, Discount code, Countdown timer |

Countdowns always use the date you set. The Discount code block shows a code with a copy button; create the code in **Discounts** in your Shopify admin first.

## Navigation

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Link lists | Columns with an image, a colored heading, a menu and a button. Up to 12 columns. | Column |

## Blog

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Blog posts | Recent posts from a blog as cards. On a blog post page it uses that post's blog and leaves the current post out. | None |

## Forms

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Contact form | A contact form with the fields you add and an optional image. An email field is always included. | Field: name, email, phone, message, custom input, dropdown, checkbox or text |
| Email signup | A newsletter signup on a color scheme or over a background image. Subscribers are added to your customers with the tag `newsletter`. | Any theme block, such as Heading, Text and Email signup, plus apps |
| Shipping calculator | Estimates shipping rates for the items in the cart. | None |

## Advanced

| Section | What it's for | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Apps | A place for app blocks anywhere on a page. | Apps, Custom Liquid |
| Custom Liquid | Your own Liquid, HTML or app snippets. The theme doesn't check this code. | None |

## Pop-up

The **Pop-up** section lives in the Overlays section group and is hidden until you turn it on. Open it from the Overlays group in the theme editor.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Type | Email signup, promotion or age verification. |
| Show | After a delay, after scrolling halfway, or when leaving the page. Age verification shows right away. |
| Don't show again for | Days before the pop-up shows again to the same visitor. |
| Only show to visitors who aren't signed in | Hides the pop-up for signed-in customers. |
| Show on mobile, Position, Width, Image | Layout options. |
| Age verification | Confirm and decline button labels, and the message after declining. |

Add blocks such as Heading, Text, Email signup and Button to the pop-up.
